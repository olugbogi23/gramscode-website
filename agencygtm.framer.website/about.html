Our Story

gramscode started after watching the same failure repeat across B2B companies. The outbound team gets hired. The sequences get written. The stack gets assembled. Six months later, the pipeline is still driven by the founder�s relationships. The diagnosis was always the same: nobody had defined the ICP with precision, and the message was describing the product instead of the buyer�s situation.

gramscode was built to fix the problem upstream of the campaign. Before the sequences. Before the messaging. Before any outreach goes live. We work with a small number of B2B companies who are serious about building a system that runs without them. We are not the right partner for everyone. But for the teams who fit, we go all in on building the map before the motion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries

Who do you work with?

We work with B2B companies that need clearer positioning, a stronger outbound system, and a more predictable pipeline. Most of our clients are SaaS, agencies, consulting firms, and service-based businesses.

How quickly can we expect results?

Most clients start seeing positive reply rates and qualified conversations within the first 30�45 days. Pipeline impact typically compounds over time as the system gets optimized.

Do you handle the outbound setup as well?

Yes. We handle everything from ICP research and messaging to domain setup, deliverability, and campaign infrastructure so your team can focus on closing deals.

Is this only for SaaS companies?

No. While we primarily work with B2B SaaS brands, we also partner with agencies, consulting firms, and other service-based businesses with high-ticket offers.

How involved does our team need to be?

Very little. We usually need a few strategy sessions upfront, then our team handles execution, optimization, and reporting while keeping you updated regularly.
